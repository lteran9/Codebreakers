THE KEEPER OF THE LIGHTHOUSE WROTE IN HIS LOG EVERY EVENING. HE NOTED THE WIND, THE HEIGHT OF THE WAVES, AND THE NAMES OF THE SHIPS THAT PASSED THE POINT BEFORE DARK. MOST NIGHTS WERE QUIET, BUT IN WINTER THE STORMS CAME FROM THE NORTH WITHOUT WARNING, AND HE WOULD CLIMB THE STAIRS TO TRIM THE LAMP UNTIL MORNING.
